# Binary

## Definition

%% recon:start definition %%
Computers only store bits, so we write numbers in base 2 or base 16 to see what's actually in memory.
%% recon:end %%

## Notes

%% recon:start from-number-systems %%
### From [[Number Systems]], 28 October 2026

- Each binary place is a power of 2: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 going right to left.
- 1101 in binary is 11 in [[Decimal|decimal]].

**Easy to get wrong**

- People often wonder why we bother with [[Hexadecimal|hex]] instead of just reading binary directly.
